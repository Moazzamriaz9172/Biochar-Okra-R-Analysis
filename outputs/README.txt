FINAL ANALYSIS OUTPUTS

from the okra mixed-wood biochar experiment.

- anova_treatment_summary.csv
- significant_responses.csv
- estimated_marginal_means.csv
- tukey_comparisons_final.csv
- treatment_means_SE.csv
- assumption_summary.csv
- treatment_ranking.csv
- overall_statistical_summary.csv

Diagnostic figures are stored in figures/diagnostics/.
Final treatment-mean figures are stored in figures/final/.
