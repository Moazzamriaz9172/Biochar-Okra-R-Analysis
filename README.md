# 🌱 Effects of Mixed-Wood Biochar on Fertilizer Use Efficiency and Growth Performance of Okra (*Abelmoschus esculentus*)
## 📖 Project Overview

Efficient nutrient management is essential for improving agricultural productivity while reducing environmental impacts. Biochar has gained attention as a sustainable soil amendment due to its ability to improve soil properties, enhance nutrient retention, and support plant growth.

This repository documents the complete analytical workflow of a greenhouse experiment investigating the effects of mixed-wood biochar on fertilizer use efficiency (FUE), growth performance, and nutrient uptake in okra (*Abelmoschus esculentus*). The project emphasizes reproducible research using R, from raw experimental data to statistical analysis and publication-quality visualizations.
## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Evaluate the effects of mixed-wood biochar on the growth and development of okra.
- Assess fertilizer use efficiency under different biochar application rates.
- Compare treatment effects using statistical analyses.
- Produce publication-quality figures and tables using R.
- Demonstrate a reproducible workflow for agricultural research.
