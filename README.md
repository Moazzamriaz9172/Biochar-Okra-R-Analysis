# 🌱 Effects of Mixed-Wood Biochar on Fertilizer Use Efficiency and Growth Performance of Okra (*Abelmoschus esculentus*)
## 📖 Project Overview

Efficient nutrient management is essential for improving agricultural productivity while reducing environmental impacts. Biochar has gained attention as a sustainable soil amendment due to its ability to improve soil properties, enhance nutrient retention, and support plant growth .

This repository documents the complete analytical workflow of a greenhouse experiment investigating the effects of mixed-wood biochar on fertilizer use efficiency (FUE), growth performance, and nutrient uptake in okra (*Abelmoschus esculentus*). The project emphasizes reproducible research using R, from raw experimental data to statistical analysis and publication-quality visualizations.
## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Evaluate the effects of mixed-wood biochar on the growth and development of okra.
- Assess fertilizer use efficiency under different biochar application rates.
- Compare treatment effects using statistical analyses.
- Produce publication-quality figures and tables using R.
- Demonstrate a reproducible workflow for agricultural research.
---

## 🧪 Experimental Design

A greenhouse pot experiment was conducted at the Nursery of MNS University of Agriculture, Multan, Pakistan, to investigate the effects of mixed-wood biochar on fertilizer use efficiency and growth performance of okra (*Abelmoschus esculentus*).

The experiment followed a **Completely Randomized Design (CRD)** consisting of **five biochar treatments** with **three replications**, resulting in **15 experimental units**.

Each earthen pot contained **8 kg of air-dried soil** collected from the experimental farm of MNS University of Agriculture. Recommended doses of NPK fertilizers were applied according to the recommendations of the Punjab Agriculture Department. Three healthy okra seedlings were transplanted into each pot and grown under uniform management practices until crop maturity.

## 📈 Statistical Workflow

The statistical analysis for this study will be performed using **R** following a reproducible workflow:

1. Import raw experimental data.
2. Data cleaning and validation.
3. Exploratory data analysis.
4. Descriptive statistics.
5. Analysis of Variance (ANOVA).
6. Tukey's HSD multiple comparison test.
7. Correlation analysis.
8. Publication-quality data visualization.
9. Interpretation of results.
